06 — Objectives

PARKORA Objectives

Five realistic engineering targets the prototype is engineered against — no marketing flourishes.
Targets

Each objective maps to a feature of the prototype and to a measurable behavior of the system.

01
Detect vehicle presence in individual parking slots using IoT sensors.
02
Display real-time parking availability via a centralized dashboard.
03
Provide floor-wise and slot-wise parking information for efficient monitoring.
04
Assist security personnel in directing vehicles to the nearest vacant spots.
05
Reduce unnecessary vehicle movement and parking search time within the facility.
