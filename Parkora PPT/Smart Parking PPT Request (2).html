03 — Diagnosis

The Problem with Conventional Parking

Three compounding failure modes are observable across most unmanaged parking facilities.
01.

Time Wastage

Drivers spend excessive time searching for vacant spaces in large, multi-floor facilities without guidance.

02.

Fuel Wastage

Unnecessary movement across multiple floors increases fuel consumption and environmental impact.

03.

Parking Congestion

Vehicles searching for spaces increase traffic density around ramps and primary parking lanes.
